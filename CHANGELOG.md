# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-18

### Changed

- Requires the 2.0 of its Laravel package, which runs on [laravel-privacy](https://github.com/jeffersongoncalves/laravel-privacy), the engine shared by the privacy suites. Same pages, settings and tables: nothing to change in your panel.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-17

19 languages: Arabic, Azerbaijani, Brazilian Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, Ukrainian and Uzbek. GDPR-specific texts (articles, lawful bases, transfer mechanisms, deadlines) are translated with each language's official GDPR terms.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-17

First release: laravel-gdpr-retention in your Filament panel. Preview what expired, anonymize it, re-apply erasure after a backup restore and browse the tombstones. English.
